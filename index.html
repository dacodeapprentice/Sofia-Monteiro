Bio

I'm a digital creator and technologist based in Porto, Portugal. My work lives at the intersection of art and code — I create immersive digital experiences that are as thoughtful in their engineering as they are in their aesthetic.

With over five years of experience spanning web development, AI integration, mobile design, and interactive installations, I bring a multidisciplinary approach to every project I undertake.

I'm open to collaborations that challenge convention — if you have an idea that blends the technical and the beautiful, I'd love to hear it.

"Art without technology is incomplete. Technology without art is cold."
