Bio

I'm [Your Name], a creative and technologist working across illustration, code, and design. My process tends to start with a question rather than a brief — what holds a pose together, why a fold resists twisting, how a story can branch without breaking.

This site exists because most of what I learn while making something never makes it into the finished thing. The archive is where that part lives — the dead ends, the half-answers, the discovery that came from the attempt that didn't quite work.

If you're curious about how something here was made, or want to build on it together, I'd love to hear from you.

"An unfinished experiment may contain the most important discovery."
